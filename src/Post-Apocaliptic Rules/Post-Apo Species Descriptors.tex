\chapter{Post-Apocayiptic Species Descriptors}\index{Post-Apocalypse!Optional Rules!Species Descriptors}

In a post-apocalyptic setting, some GMs may want to offer species affected by the disaster.

\section{Morlock}

You have lived your life deep underground in artificial bunkers, hidden from the world’s destruction and the brutal scavengers that live above. As a morlock, you have a keen mind for the technology salvaged from the before-time. In fact, every morlock comes of age by fitting a piece of morlock technology to its body to provide enhancement and extend its life. This means that you are part flesh and part machine. Your skin is as pale as milk, except where it’s been replaced with strips of metal and glowing circuits.

You gain the following characteristics:

\textbf{Enhanced Intelligence}: +2 to your Intellect Pool. 

\textbf{Cyborg Body}: +2 to your Might Pool and your Speed Pool.

\textbf{Partially Metallic}: +1 to Armor.

\textbf{Repair and Maintenance}: As an entity of living flesh and humming machinery, you must first succeed on a difficulty 2 repair task before making a recovery roll. On a failure, the recovery roll is not used; however, the normal rules for retrying apply, and you must use Effort on a new roll if you wish to try again. In addition to the normal options for using Effort, you can choose to use Effort to heal additional points to your Pools (each level of Effort healing an additional 2 points to your Pools if you succeed).

\textbf{Morlock Prejudice}: While among non-morlocks, all positive interaction tasks are hindered.

Initial Link to the Starting Adventure: From the following list of options, choose how you became involved in the first adventure.
\begin{itemize}
\item The PCs found you in a collapsed subterranean tunnel.
\item The other PCs encountered you exploring underground, and you convinced them to allow you to accompany them.
\item You were exiled from the morlock communities and needed help on the surface.
\item The only way to save the morlock community you hail from is to venture to the surface and find a mechanical part needed to repair a failing ancient system.
\end{itemize}

\section{Roach}

You are born of a species of evolved insects once called “cockroach,” but that is far in the past. Radiation and forced evolution have radically increased your size, shape, and ability to think. Your exoskeleton mimics the shape of a human being, though not perfectly. When you move about human society, shadows and cloaks are your ally if you wish to pass unnoticed. When those of your kind are discovered, it usually goes poorly for someone. You, however, have a wandering spirit and seek to explore the fallen world and find a new way forward.

You gain the following characteristics:

\textbf{Scuttler}: Your Speed Edge increases by 1.

\textbf{Sense by Scent}: You can sense your environment even in total darkness. 

\textbf{Cling}: You can move an immediate distance each round on walls or clinging to the ceiling.

\textbf{Carapace}: +1 to Armor.

\textbf{Glide}: You can extend small wings from your carapace that grant an asset in jumping tasks and allow you to fall up to a short distance without taking damage.

\textbf{Skill}: You are trained in disguise tasks.

\textbf{Inability}: You are susceptible to disease and poison. Defense rolls against disease or poison are hindered.

\textbf{Inability}: You mimic a human, but you are not as fierce. Tasks involving combat— including attack and defense rolls—are hindered.

\textbf{Insect Prejudice}: While among non-roaches, all positive interaction tasks are hindered.

Initial Link to the Starting Adventure: From the following list of options, choose how you became involved in the first adventure.
\begin{itemize}
\item The PCs didn’t realize what you were when they asked for your help.
\item You’ve managed to hide your roach ancestry so well that everyone thinks you are like them.
\item You are the last of your kind.
\item You have a secret agenda, and the PCs were gullible enough to let you come along.
\end{itemize}
