\chapter{Superhero Artifacts}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by genre!Superhero}

Supervillains build doomsday devices. Ancient artifacts present a threat to all humanity if in the wrong hands. Weird machines from alien dimensions offer solutions to unsolvable problems. Artifacts are an important part of superhero stories. A few examples are below.

\section{Doctor Dread's Time Portal}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by genre!Superhero!Doctor' Dread's Time Portal}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by name!Doctor' Dread's Time Portal}

\textbf{Level}: 9

\textbf{Form}: Arch of metal big enough to walk through

\textbf{Effect}: Anyone who steps through it goes to a predetermined point in the past or future (a minimum of fifty years in either direction), which can be anywhere on the planet.

\textbf{Depletion}: 1 in 1d20

\section{Serum X}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by genre!Superhero!Serum X}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by name!Serum X}

\textbf{Level}: 1d6 + 2

\textbf{Form}: Vial or syringe of red fluid

\textbf{Effect}: Strips someone of all superpowers (including abilities granted by magic, psionics,
mutation, or science) for twenty-four hours. The target retains only skills and abilities that are mundane, as agreed by the GM and player.

\textbf{Depletion}: Automatic

\section{Stellarex Crystal}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by genre!Superhero!Stellarex Crystal}
\index[CnA]{Artifacts by name!Stellarex Crystal}

\textbf{Level}: 1d6 + 4

\textbf{Form}: Multifaceted purple stone the size of a fist

\textbf{Effect}: Created in the dawning of the universe, this artifact grants the wielder the ability to not only fully restore all their stat Pools, but also increase each Pool temporarily by 10 points. These extra points fade after twenty-four hours if not used.

\textbf{Depletion}: 1–3 in 1d10
