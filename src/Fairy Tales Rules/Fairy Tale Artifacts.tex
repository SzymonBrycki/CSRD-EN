\chapter{Fairy Tale Artifact}\index{Fairy Tales!Artifacts}

Artifacts in a fantasy setting and magic items in other games focused on fantasy would also be suitable for a fairy tale setting. However, every fairy tale artifact should come with a quirk that sets it apart from a simple “wand of fire” or similar item. Come up with your own or roll a quirk on the table below.

\end{multicols}

\begin{table}[h]

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{| Y | Y |}
\hline

\textbf{d10} & \textbf{Quirk} \\ \hline

1 & Is sometimes invisible. \\ \hline
2 & Cries like a baby if jostled. \\ \hline
3 & Becomes cold as ice to the touch and emits cold vapor when danger threatens. \\ \hline
4 & Contains a secret compartment that invariably holds a chunk of rock broken from what might be a strange jade sculpture. \\ \hline
5 & Also serves as a key to some magically locked doors and chests. \\ \hline
6 & Bites owner with tiny teeth if jostled, dealing 1 point of damage. \\ \hline
7 & Always muttering and complaining, though useful warnings and other information can sometimes be gained. \\ \hline
8 & Jealous of any other manifest cyphers, artifacts, or beautiful objects in the wielder’s life. \\ \hline
9 & The “painting” of a princess of summer on the object sometimes leaves it, robbing the artifact of power. \\ \hline
10 & Causes flowers to grow wherever it is stored or set down. \\ \hline

\end{tabularx}

\end{table}
