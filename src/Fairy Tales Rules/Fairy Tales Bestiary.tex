\section{Basic Creatures and NPCs for a Fairy Tale Game}\index{Creatures and NPC!Fairy Tales!Basic}

Most fey creatures of level 2 or higher regain 1 point of health per round, unless wounded by silvered or cold iron weapons.

\textbf{Angry ants}: swarm as a level 1 creature; constantly whisper insults, slurs, and obscenities; those physically attacked must also succeed on a difficulty 3 Might defense task or be stunned and lose their next turn

\textbf{Erlking}: level 6, stealth as level 7; health 27; Armor 4; short-range whisper attack enthralls target for one hour or until attacked; root tendril attacks on up to three separate targets in immediate range; silvered and cold iron weapons ignore the erlking’s Armor

(Erlking: An animated accumulation of woodland debris—bark, lost teeth, matted weeds, and dirt—that wears a crown of oak leaves and a cloak of mist.)

\textbf{Faerie}: level 4, deception and Speed defense as level 5; short-range magic dust attack inflicts damage or makes target amenable to faerie suggestions for one minute

\textbf{Feral tree}: level 3; Armor 3; rooted in place; lashing branches attack up to three characters as a single action and on a failed Might defense task, hold the victim in place until they can escape

\textbf{Nymph}: level 3, stealth and positive social interactions as level 6

\textbf{Pixie}: level 2, stealth and finding lost items as level 6

\textbf{Razorblade butterflies}: level 1; swarm as a level 3 creature able to attack all creatures in an area an immediate distance across

\textbf{Talking cat}: level 1, knowledge tasks as level 7

\textbf{Troll}: level 6; claws inflict 7 points of damage and grab victim until they escape; grabbed creature takes 10 points of damage per round; troll regains 3 points of health per round
